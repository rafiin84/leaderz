# Working preferences

## Corrections: just do them

When I ask for a correction, apply it directly. Don't deliberate, don't
re-litigate the design, don't ask which of two readings I meant — pick the
obvious one and make the change.

This covers the everyday stuff: colours, spacing, borders, backgrounds, sizes,
labels, moving or removing a section, swapping an icon, renaming a heading.

**Keep the reply short.** One or two lines saying what changed. No tables of
verification results, no lists of considered alternatives, no closing offers of
further work I didn't ask for.

## What still deserves a pause

Don't take "don't think" as licence to skip the parts that protect the work:

- **Still verify the change worked.** Quietly. Just don't narrate it at length.
- **Still say so if something breaks**, or if a change has a consequence I
  clearly wouldn't want (data lost on mobile, a section made unreachable, an
  action that can't be undone).
- **Still ask** when the request genuinely can't be acted on — a missing file,
  a credential you don't have, two readings that would produce completely
  different work.

The rule is "skip the deliberation", not "skip the care".

## Commits

Keep committing and pushing after each meaningful change, as `CLAUDE.md` says.
Commit messages can stay detailed — that's the record. It's the chat reply that
should be brief.
